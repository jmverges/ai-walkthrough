Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

"AI Walkthrough uploader" is a personal tool used by the owner of the AI Walkthrough YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent it uses two Google permissions: uploading videos (youtube.upload) and reading the channel's basic details (youtube.readonly), only to confirm which channel it uploads to.

What it stores

An OAuth refresh token, kept on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner's user account. It is never shared, sold or sent anywhere other than Google's own servers. No data about any other person is collected.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, and uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
